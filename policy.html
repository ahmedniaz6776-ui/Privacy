1Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the Zilnashd VIP Girls Hostel Management App ("the App"), developed and operated by Zilnashd Hotel ("we", "us", "our"), collects, uses, stores, and protects information when used by hostel administrators (the "Admin"), residents ("Students"), and, indirectly, hostel staff whose records the Admin maintains inside the App.

The App is a private, permission-based hostel management tool — it is not an open public consumer application. A Student account can only be created after the hostel Admin has first approved that Student's registered email address inside the system; there is no unrestricted public sign-up. By using the Admin Panel or the Student Portal, you acknowledge that this Policy governs how your information is collected and handled.

2Information We Collect

2.1 Account & Authentication Information

  • Admin accounts: email address and password, used solely to sign in to the Admin Panel via Firebase Authentication.
  • Student accounts: full name, email address, and password, used to sign in to the Student Portal via Firebase Authentication. Sign-up is only possible for an email address the Admin has already pre-approved.
  • Passwords are never stored or visible to us in plain text — they are handled entirely by Firebase Authentication's own secure credential system.
  • Email verification links and password-reset links are sent through Firebase Authentication's built-in email service. We do not operate a separate custom OTP/SMS system.

2.2 Student Personal & Guardian Information

  • Full name, CNIC / B-Form number, mobile number, home address.
  • Father's / guardian's name, guardian's CNIC number, and an emergency contact number.
  • Room number, admission date, and (limited to just her roommates' names, never their phone numbers) roommate information.

This information is entered into the system by the Admin during registration and is used strictly for hostel administration purposes such as identity verification, room allocation, and emergency contact.

2.3 Financial & Fee Information

  • Hostel dues, electricity dues, mess fees, fines, discounts, and previous balances.
  • Payment history: amount received, remaining balance, a manually-entered payment method label (e.g. "Cash", "Bank Transfer", "EasyPaisa/JazzCash"), payment date, receipt number, and optional transaction reference/remarks.
The App does not integrate any online payment gateway, card processor, or mobile-wallet API. All payments are collected offline by hostel staff and simply recorded in the system by the Admin for bookkeeping and receipt-generation purposes.

2.4 Complaints

When a Student submits a complaint, we store the complaint category, a free-text description, its resolution status, the submission date, and the identifying details (name, room, account email) needed to route it to the correct Admin.

2.5 Staff & Inventory Records (Admin-Only)

The Admin may separately store hostel staff records (name, role, mobile number, CNIC, salary, joining date, address, duty shift, employment status) and inventory/asset records (item name, category, room location, quantity, condition). This information is entered and viewed only by the Admin and is never visible inside the Student Portal.

2.6 Device & Security Information (Admin Only)

  • To limit an Admin account to a maximum of four signed-in devices, we store a device label derived from the browser's technical information (e.g. "Android Device", "iPhone (Safari)"), the operating platform, and link/last-active timestamps.
  • A randomly generated device identifier is also stored locally in that device's browser storage to recognize it on future logins.
  • When the Admin links a new device using a QR code, a short-lived, single-use, randomly generated security token is created that automatically expires after 90 seconds.

2.7 Notices & App Preferences

Hostel-wide notices authored by the Admin (title and body text) are stored and shown to residents. Cosmetic interface preferences (such as background animation, sound effects, or reduced-motion toggles) are saved only in the Admin's own browser local storage and are never transmitted to our servers.

2.8 Automatically Collected Technical Information

Like any website, standard technical information (such as IP address and browser/user-agent string) is incidentally visible to the infrastructure providers that deliver the App's content — namely our hosting provider, our database/authentication provider, and the content-delivery networks that serve front-end libraries. We do not separately log, analyze, or process this information ourselves within the App.

What we do NOT collect: we do not use any analytics or advertising SDKs, we do not access your device's contacts, SMS, precise location, or photo gallery, and we do not permanently store or transmit any camera image — the camera is used only briefly and locally to read a QR code during the Admin's device-linking flow, and no video or image is ever saved.

3How We Use Information

  • Authentication and account security — to verify identity, issue password resets, and enforce a maximum device limit for Admin logins.
  • Hostel administration — to manage room allocation, roommate assignment, and resident records.
  • Billing and fee management — to track dues, record payments, and generate/view digital receipts.
  • Complaint handling — to let residents report issues and let the Admin track and resolve them.
  • Internal operations — to help the Admin manage staff records and hostel inventory.
  • Communication — to publish hostel-wide notices to residents, and (if a user has granted browser notification permission) to deliver push notifications.
  • App functionality and performance — to remember interface preferences and enable faster, partially offline-capable loading of the App.

We do not use personal information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to operating the hostel management system.

4Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. Information is only accessible in the following ways:

  • Within your own hostel account: an Admin can view and manage the full records of the Students, staff, and fee data belonging to their own hostel account. A Student can view only her own profile, room/roommate names, fee history, complaints, and hostel notices — she cannot see other residents' personal or financial records.
  • Infrastructure/processing partners: data is stored and processed using Firebase Authentication and Firebase Realtime Database (operated by Google LLC), which act as our data processors for authentication and database storage.
  • Push notification service: if a user grants browser notification permission, limited subscription information is shared with OneSignal, our push-notification service provider.
  • Static hosting and content delivery: the App's front-end files are served through Netlify (hosting) and standard content-delivery networks (used only to load public front-end libraries such as fonts/icons/scripts), which may incidentally receive standard web request information like an IP address.

We may also disclose information where required to comply with a valid legal request or to protect the rights, property, or safety of the hostel, its residents, or the public.

5Data Security

  • All communication between the App and our database/authentication provider uses standard encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections.
  • Passwords are managed entirely by Firebase Authentication's secure credential system — we never handle or view plain-text passwords.
  • The database structure is organized so that each Admin account's hostel data is stored under its own account-scoped path, and a Student's data is only accessible from within her own linked profile.
  • Admin device-linking uses a time-limited (90-second), single-use security token so that a new device can only be authorized once, and the Admin can review and revoke any previously linked device at any time from the Admin Profile screen.

While we take reasonable steps to protect information, no method of electronic storage or transmission is ever 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6Data Retention

  • Student profile data, roommate mirrors, and complaint records are retained for as long as the Student's account and hostel residency remain active, or until the Admin or the Student deletes them (see Account Deletion below).
  • Fee and payment ledger entries created by the Admin are retained by the Admin for hostel accounting/record-keeping purposes even after a Student's own account is deleted, in the same way a physical hostel accounts register would be kept. Only the Student's personal, read-only copy of her fee history is removed when she deletes her account.
  • Staff and inventory records are retained until the Admin manually removes them.
  • Device-linking security tokens automatically expire and are effectively unusable after 90 seconds.
  • Locally stored device identifiers and interface preferences remain only on that specific browser/device until its local storage is cleared.

7Account Deletion

7.1 Student Self-Service Deletion

A Student can permanently delete her own account at any time from Student Portal → Profile → Danger Zone → Delete Account. Confirming this action will:

  • Permanently remove her personal profile record (name, CNIC, mobile, address, guardian/emergency information, room assignment).
  • Remove her roommate-mirror entry, her own complaint history, and her personal (read-only) copy of her fee/payment history.
  • Release her email address from the pre-approved signup list.
  • Permanently delete her Firebase Authentication login (email and password), so she will not be able to sign in again with that account.

The Admin's own accounting ledger entries relating to past fee/payment transactions are not automatically erased by this action, as explained in Data Retention above, since these serve as the hostel's own financial records.

7.2 Admin-Initiated Removal

An Admin can remove a Student's profile record directly from the Admin Panel. This deletes that Student's stored personal/room data and releases her approved email, but it does not, by itself, delete the Student's underlying login credentials — those remain deletable only through the Student's own self-service option described above, or through a direct request to the hostel administration.

7.3 Admin Accounts

There is currently no in-app self-service option for an Admin to delete their own account. An Admin who wishes to close their account should contact the hostel administration directly using the process described in Contact Us.

8Children's Privacy

The App is an internal administrative tool used within a residential hostel setting, and it is possible that some residents recorded in the system are minors (reflected, for example, by the "CNIC / B-Form" identity field used in Pakistan for individuals under 18). The App does not offer open, unsupervised public sign-up to anyone, including children: every Student account can only be created after the hostel Admin — acting as the responsible institutional authority — has first approved that resident's email address inside the system.

Any information relating to a resident who is a minor is entered and managed under the direct supervision of the hostel administration for legitimate hostel-administration purposes (identity verification, room allocation, emergency contact, and billing), rather than being collected directly from a child through public self-registration.

9User Rights & Choices

  • Access: Students can view their own profile, room, fee, and complaint information at any time from within the Student Portal.
  • Correction: since personal/room details are entered by the Admin, corrections to profile information should be requested through the hostel Admin.
  • Deletion: Students may permanently delete their own account and associated personal data at any time, as described in Account Deletion.
  • Notifications: push notification permission can be withdrawn at any time from your device's or browser's own notification settings.
  • Local preferences: cosmetic interface preferences stored in your browser can be removed by clearing that browser's site data.
